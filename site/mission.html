public The Core Mandate
“Pharma Corridor covers how the world makes medicine: the manufacturing, quality, supply, and policy moving drugs between the places that make them and the places that need them. We over-index on India, where much of that work happens, and report every corridor connected to it.”

Global healthcare resilience depends on opaque, transnational physical networks. When an API synthesis hub in Hyderabad experiences a raw-material interruption or a sterile injectable suite in New Jersey receives a critical Form 483, the clinical shockwaves cascade across the European medicines agencies, US hospital systems, and global tender boards. We exist to provide unvarnished, data-grounded transparency across this critical arterial infrastructure.
