MENA

A large, fast-growing market that still imports most of its medicine, and a region determined to change that. Gulf sovereign wealth and North African manufacturing are driving a localization push from Riyadh to Cairo.

The hubs

A corridor made of several, not one

Saudi Arabia

~36% of the Middle East
largest market · localization engine

The region's biggest market and the centre of the localization drive. Vision 2030 is pushing it away from roughly 70-80% import dependence toward domestic and contract manufacturing, with the SFDA regulating and NUPCO running procurement. Multinationals are being drawn into local production and joint ventures, from generics to plasma.

United Arab Emirates

~$5.0B · 2025
fast-growing · regional hub

The third-largest market and among the fastest-growing, doubling as a logistics and re-export gateway. Government policy targets a 40% expansion in local production by 2026.

Egypt

exports to 147 markets
largest by population · North Africa's maker

The biggest market by population and North Africa's largest manufacturer, exporting to 147 markets, mostly within MENA and Africa. Yet it still imports over 90% of its active ingredients, and is pushing from fill-and-finish toward deeper manufacturing.

Türkiye, Jordan, Morocco & Algeria

manufacturing & generics export

Established manufacturing and export bases. Jordan is a long-standing regional generics exporter; the Maghreb increasingly protective of local production.

The Gulf shared system

GCC registration & tenders

A joint registration process and pooled tenders across the Gulf states, where products already cleared by the FDA or EMA move faster.

Each figure is for a single market. Market-size scope differs between MENA, the Middle East, and the Middle East and Africa, and is labelled accordingly.

How the corridor works

Position
A large, fast-growing regional market of around $60B that still imports most of its medicine, now pushing hard to manufacture at home.
Flows in
The defining flow. Most markets import 70-95% of finished medicine and active ingredients, from India, Europe, the US and increasingly China.
Flows out
Limited but growing. Egypt and Jordan export generics across MENA and Africa; the Gulf is starting to build export-oriented capacity.
Largest markets
Saudi Arabia (about 36% of the Middle East), then Türkiye and the UAE; Egypt is the largest by population.
Localization drive
Vision 2030 in Saudi Arabia, the UAE's 40%-by-2026 target, Egypt's deep-manufacturing push and protective procurement across the Maghreb.
Regulator gates
Saudi Arabia's SFDA, the UAE authorities and Egypt's EDA, plus GCC shared registration. FDA or EMA approval speeds entry.
Open question
Whether the localization drive converts a large import bill into real domestic capacity, and how Gulf sovereign investment reshapes who supplies the region.
On the structure
Like Rest of World, MENA is a grouping of markets rather than a single trade entity. If the Gulf build-out delivers, Saudi Arabia or the GCC could graduate into a corridor of its own.
