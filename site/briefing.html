Frequently Asked Questions

How does team and enterprise licensing work? expand_more

Enterprise memberships provide unlimited or multi-tier seat allocation linked via Single Sign-On (SSO) or corporate domain whitelisting. Procurement departments receive a single consolidated Master Services Agreement (MSA) with Net 30/60 terms.

Can we integrate customs trade flows directly into our internal ERP or data lake? expand_more

Yes. Corridor Terminal enterprise seats include secure REST and GraphQL API tokens to pipe bill-of-lading data, tariff rates, and inspection OCR alerts directly into internal ERP risk engines (SAP, Oracle, Palantir).

Why is the Daily Briefing completely free? expand_more

The executive wire serves as our institutional flagship, establishing our analytical viewpoint across the industry. Our commercial model is supported entirely by quantitative terminal software subscriptions and enterprise research dossiers—not advertisers or pharmaceutical marketing grants.

What is your methodology for Form 483 and inspection surveillance? expand_more

Our algorithmic monitors poll public and proprietary FOIA repositories across 42 global regulatory bodies every 15 minutes. Documents are parsed through specialized OCR to extract inspector patterns, citation repeat counts, and cross-reference affected Drug Master Files (DMFs).
