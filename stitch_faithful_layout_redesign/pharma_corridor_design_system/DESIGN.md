## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, high-density editorial intelligence platform tailored for executive leadership, scientific researchers, regulatory analysts, and investors in the global biopharmaceutical sector. The brand evokes institutional gravitas, clinical precision, and urgent market clarity—reminiscent of premier global financial broadsheets combined with high-grade quantitative data infrastructure.

The design movement is **Refined Analytical Editorial**. It intentionally eschews generic SaaS tropes, decorative gradients, and floating drop shadows in favor of a crisp typographic grid, disciplined 1px hairline wire channels, high-contrast dark navy anchors, and subtle tinted structural panels. Visual hierarchy is established through meticulous type sizing, calibrated font weights (notably regular `400` and book-medium `450`), and structured tabular rows that prioritize scan-speed and analytical depth.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict light mode framework anchored by high-contrast pharmaceutical navy and crisp editorial white. 

- **Primary (`#0B2A52`)**: The definitive brand anchor used for primary feature modules, executive briefing containers, high-emphasis interactive states, navigation leads, and key data points.
- **Secondary (`#1C3E72`)**: Interactive hover and active focus partner for primary fills.
- **Tertiary (`#EDF1F8`)**: Pale cool blue wash for structural panel breaks, cell highlight states, and section groupings.
- **Neutral (`#151A22`)**: Pure editorial ink, ensuring maximum legibility across clinical and financial documentation.

### Semantic & Surface System
- **Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: The pristine primary reading surface where main body content and white data cells reside.
- **Panel Break (`#F5F7FB`)**: Secondary neutral tone for nested column groups, metadata blocks, and distinct editorial lists.
- **Muted (`#5B6472`) and Faint (`#949AA6`)**: Secondary and tertiary ink tiers applied strictly to meta lines, timestamps, tickers, datelines, and navigation links.
- **Lines & Wire Dividers**: Separators utilize `#E7E9EF` for ultra-fine hairlines and `#D3D8E1` for mid-tone borders, form controls, and active dividers.
- **Inverted Dark Surfaces**: When modules use navy surfaces, foreground typography flips to `#FFFFFF` for primary indicators and `#A9B6D0` for secondary metadata. Risk or regulatory indicators utilize `#B07515`.

## Typography

The typography is built entirely on **Hanken Grotesk**, producing a cohesive, crisp, and analytical presence across all scales. Sizing and weights are calibrated to match the authority of print broadsheets.

- **Weight Nuance**: Distinctive utilization of weight `450` for secondary headers, feature callouts, and article row titles creates an intermediate density between standard body text (`400`) and functional structural labels (`500`/`600`).
- **Tabular Figures**: Numeric data points, regulatory dates, flow rankings, and tracker numbers must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure strict vertical alignment across comparative rows.
- **Section Headers & Eyebrows**: Sized at `12.5px` with uppercase treatment, `600` weight, and `+0.02em` tracking, paired with subtle underline bars to demarcate thematic sections.
- **Reading Measure**: Editorial prose containers enforce a strict reading measure between `60ch` and `66ch` (max 660px) to safeguard legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed as a structured fixed-width system with a strict max container width of `1080px` (`--maxw`). It is centered with horizontal margins adjusting dynamically: `32px` on desktop screen widths, scaling down to `20px` on mobile displays (≤820px).

### The Grid Framework
- **Editorial Lead Grid**: An asymmetric two-column split (`1.35fr` to `0.65fr`) with a `56px` gutter on desktop, collapsing cleanly into a stacked `1fr` column with `32px` gaps on mobile.
- **Intelligence Metric Grid**: 4-column metric blocks with `1px` structural hairline dividers on desktop, shifting into a balanced 2x2 matrix on tablet and mobile viewports.
- **Corridor & List Channels**: 3-column directory grids on desktop (`repeat(3, 1fr)`) converting to 2-column or single-stack on constrained viewports. Rather than margin gaps, card matrices share seamless `1px` recessed gutter lines created by parent border backgrounds.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Major analytical blocks are separated by `52px` to `64px` vertical margins. Inner list rows maintain rhythmic compact heights (`16px` to `20px` internal padding) for dense visual scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects skeuomorphic drop shadows and heavy multi-tiered elevation stacks. Visual depth is rendered through flat, architectural surface stratification and hairline delineation.

- **Hairline Framing**: Separation between content layers is handled via `1px solid #E7E9EF` hairlines and structural `1px` track gaps.
- **Tonal Contrast Stacking**: Hierarchy is established by alternating clean white fields (`#FFFFFF`), muted intelligence panels (`#F5F7FB`), and solid anchor blocks (`#0B2A52`). Deep navy units serve as functional visual bookends.
- **Transient Overlays**: Depth via shadow is reserved strictly for ephemeral interaction layers:
  - Dropdown Menus: Sits on `box-shadow: 0 12px 34px rgba(16, 38, 69, 0.12)` with a `1px solid #E7E9EF` border.
  - Mobile Drawer Navigation: Elevated with `box-shadow: 0 14px 30px rgba(16, 38, 69, 0.12)`.
- **Frosted Translucency**: The sticky masthead utilizes an editorial glass filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`) over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.86)` with a `1px` bottom hairline, maintaining continuous spatial context during deep document scrolling.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances clinical, squared-off structural architecture with surgical pill-shaped interactive micro-components.

- **Structural Containers**: Analytical panels, summary blocks, and modular list groups feature modest, refined corners (`12px` to `14px`), avoiding roundness that would conflict with tabular data.
- **Flyout Menus & Tooltips**: Small utility menus, dropdown items, and search suggest popouts maintain compact `4px` to `6px` radii.
- **Interactive Controls & Badges**: Action items, including primary pill buttons, newsletter email inputs, filter chips, and category badges, utilize complete pill styling (`rounded-full` / `999px`).
- **Data Bars**: Mini progress tracks and indicator bars within flow diagrams use precise, micro-radii of `3px`.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Feature Button (`.briefing-btn`)**: Fully pill-shaped (`999px`), deep navy background (`#0B2A52`), pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `13px` font size with `500` weight, and smooth hover transition to `#1C3E72`.
- **Inline Action Link (`.tw-all`)**: `14px` size, `500` weight, text color `#0B2A52` with a discrete `1px solid #0B2A52` underline anchor.
- **Form Submit Actions**: Integrated within pill input fields, providing seamless borderless integration (`.tw-cta-form .go`).

### Filter Chips & Tags
- **Micro Chips (`.chip`)**: Pill-shaped (`999px`), `13px` type with `450` weight, padding `4px 12px`.
  - Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D3D8E1`, text `#5B6472`.
  - Active: Background `#0B2A52`, border `1px solid #0B2A52`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Editorial Badges (`.tw-badge`)**: `12px` size, `500` weight, pill shape, soft tint background (`#EDF1F8`), and navy text (`#0B2A52`).

### Editorial Wire Rows & Lists
- **Article Stream Rows (`.row`)**: Stacked rows separated by `1px solid #E7E9EF` borders. Features vertical padding of `28px`, headline at `19px` (weight `450`), and `14.5px` standfirst in `#5B6472`.
- **Track & Flow Rows (`.trackrow`, `.destrow`)**: Dense tabular lists with fixed-width label columns (`70px` to `190px`), flexible detail copy, and embedded quantitative progress meters utilizing a `#E7E9EF` background track with `#0B2A52` or `#C3CEE0` fills.

### Cards & Modular Grids
- **Corridor Grid Cell (`.cor-cell`)**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), internal padding `26px 24px 30px`. Separated via `1px` recessed hairline grid tracks. Transitions background to `#EDF1F8` on hover in `0.18s`.
- **Metric Highlight Cell (`.ncell`)**: Nested within navy modules; pure `#FFFFFF` primary numerical values (`32px` weight `500`) paired with uppercase muted labels in `#A9B6D0`.

### Form Fields
- **Search & Newsletter Inputs**: Sized at `13.5px`, surrounded by a pill-shaped container, with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #D3D8E1` perimeter stroke, and ink text `#151A22`. Clear focus states enforce `outline: 2px solid #0B2A52` with `3px` offset.
